## Priority 1: Strategic Analysis Tools (Complete)

### 1. backlog_filter
**File**: `backlog_filter_skill.md`  
**Purpose**: Prioritize work by clarity of understanding, not size/complexity  
**Input**: Work items (titles + descriptions)  
**Output**: Categorized items (High/Medium/Low clarity), sequenced priorities, discovery plans  
**Key Principle**: Large + clear is safer than small + ambiguous

### 2. strategy_ecosystem_mapper
**File**: `strategy_ecosystem_mapper_skill.md`  
**Purpose**: Map competitive landscapes using Corporate Forest metaphor  
**Input**: Organization description, competitors, market context  
**Output**: Ecosystem map, position hypothesis, strategic opportunities  
**Key Principle**: Metaphor democratizes strategy — enables non-experts to engage meaningfully

### 3. premortem_analyzer
**File**: `premortem_analyzer_skill.md`  
**Purpose**: Systematic risk analysis through imagined future failure  
**Input**: Project/initiative description, timeline, objectives  
**Output**: Failure scenarios, root causes, prevention strategies, prioritized actions  
**Key Principle**: Assume failure happened to bypass optimism bias
